      Preprints and dissertations

      Preprints come from EdArXiv (STEM+ preprints only) arXiv Physics Education, and arXiv History and Philosophy of Physics. Preprints have not been peer reviewed; one that is later published in a followed journal is listed once, as the journal article. Dissertations and theses come from university repositories through OpenAlex, limited to English-language work in STEM+ education, history and philosophy of science, and science and health communication topics; OpenAlex does not separate doctoral dissertations from master's theses.

        Journal listings come from Crossref; preprints from the OSF and arXiv; dissertations from OpenAlex. STEMEd+ Commons listings come from Knowledge Commons Works. Titles link to the original work; abstracts are not copied.

        Keywords are terms from HuMeta, a metadata profile for STEM+ education research, each linked to ERIC Thesaurus descriptors. They are matched in titles, in abstracts that publishers and preprint servers share, and in author and STEMEd+ Commons keywords, so an article can be missing a keyword that fits it.
